# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Assuming positive intent
- Giving and accepting feedback professionally

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments and contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.
